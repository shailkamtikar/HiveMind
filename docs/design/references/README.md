## What these have in common (the "not AI slop" pattern)
- Warm off-white background, near-black text; **one** accent color used sparingly (a single phrase, one button, one bar).
- Headlines are the design: big, tightly set, with one word or phrase emphasized (color or italic).
- Small mono/uppercase labels, hairline rules, numbering and captions give an editorial or technical-document feel.
- Show the real product or a real diagram; no gradient blobs, no glassmorphism, no emoji icons, no identical three-card grids.
